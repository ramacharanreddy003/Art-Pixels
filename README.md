# Pencils & Pixels

Personal creative portfolio of **Rama Charan Reddy Chinnakambaiah Gari** — showcasing pencil sketches and nature photography.

🔗 **Live site:** [ramacharanreddy003.github.io/Pencils-and-Pixels](https://ramacharanreddy003.github.io/Pencils-and-Pixels/)

## About

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate based in Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, with a passion for both technology and creativity. Alongside my interest in software development, I enjoy creating realistic pencil sketches and capturing the beauty of nature through photography. This site is where I showcase that work.

## Sections

- **About Me** — a short introduction and profile photo
- **Art Gallery** — a collection of pencil sketches
- **Photography** — nature, temple, and landscape photography
- **Contact** — links to email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and Instagram

## Built With

- HTML5 & CSS3 (responsive grid layout, no framework)
- [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) (Poppins)
- [Font Awesome](https://fontawesome.com/) & [Bootstrap Icons](https://icons.getbootstrap.com/) for icons
- Images hosted on [Cloudinary](https://cloudinary.com/) and GitHub raw content
- Hosted for free on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/)
